> [!question] How visualize more than 4 attributes and look for correlation?
> Using x,y,z and color grading we can show at maximum 4 dimensions 


> [!Success] PCA transforms the data into a new coordinate system where
> 1. The first principal component (PC1) captures the maximum variance in the data.
> 2. The second principal component (PC2) captures the next highest variance, orthogonal to PC1.
> 3. This process continues for all components, ranking them by their variance contribution.
> 4. We then keep only the top **k** components, reducing dimensionality while retaining key information.
