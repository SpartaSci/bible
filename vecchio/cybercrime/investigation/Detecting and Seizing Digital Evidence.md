### Correctly Extracting Digital Evidence:
1. **Creating a bit-stream copy**:
   - Sequential duplication of the entire physical medium.
   - Creates a physical image identical to the original disk.
   - Prevents tampering with the original evidence.

2. **Validating the copy**:
   - Use hash functions to ensure data integrity.
   - The hash serves as a "digital fingerprint," detecting accidental or intentional changes.

### Common Challenges:
- Evidence can reside on suspect devices or third parties (e.g., ISPs, banks, email providers, etc.).
- Collecting data from the cloud is complex: data is often scattered globally.
