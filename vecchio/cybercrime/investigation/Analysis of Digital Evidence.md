

- **Bitstream Copy**:
  - Slower but allows repeatable analysis.
  - Requires attorneys’ presence to follow best practices.
- **Live Forensics**:
  - Faster but does not allow repeatable analysis.
  - Used when bitstream copies are not feasible (e.g., due to jurisdiction or encryption).
### Search Methods:
- **Text Searches**: To scan files, directories, and file systems for specific terms.
- **Image Searches**: To identify image files and extract frames from digital videos.
- **Data Recovery**: To recover deleted or damaged files.
- **Data Discovery**: Access encrypted, protected, or hidden data.
- **Data Carving**: Reconstruct damaged files by retrieving portions of their content.
- **Metadata Recovery**: Generate timelines and logs to reconstruct actions.

### Use of Artificial Intelligence:
- AI can accelerate searches but cannot generate evidence, as AI-generated evidence is inadmissible in court.

### Tool Selection:
- **Open Source**: Publicly testable and generally less prone to errors.
- **Closed Source**: Proprietary code, more vulnerable to issues.
