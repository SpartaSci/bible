

The general approach to identifying a suspect begins when an investigator:
- Receives a complaint from a victim of cybercrime, or
- Detects illegal content online.

### Procedure:
1. **Obtaining the physical location of the IP**:
   - Through the court system, the investigator requests the ISP (Internet Service Provider) to provide:
     - The physical location of a dynamic IP (using a timestamp and IP data).
     - Note: Static IPs do not require the provider's intervention for location discovery.

2. **Search warrant**:
   - Once the location is identified, a warrant authorizes the search and seizure of devices for analysis.

### Data Retention:
- Data retention (CDRs, IPDRs) is critical to identifying or preventing serious crimes.
- Retention periods vary:
  - Italy: 5 years.
  - Germany: 0 years.
- **Example of avoidance**: Using TOR renders data untraceable.

### SOCMINT and OSINT:
- **SOCMINT (Social Media Intelligence)** and **OSINT (Open Source Intelligence)** are used in early phases to identify suspects.
- **Ethical considerations**: In countries like China, freedom is sacrificed for security through social control.
