

### Identified or Identifiable Natural Person
^980253

- **Identified Person**:  
    An individual is **identified** when personal data directly points to their identity, such as a **name**, **identification number**, or **address**. This means the person can be recognized and distinguished from others using this information.
    
- **Identifiable Person**:  
    An individual is **identifiable** if, even though they are not directly identified, they can be **singled out** by certain information. Through further research or combining data (like an IP address, location data, or online identifier), it would be possible to determine the person's identity.
