> The **Organisation for Economic Co-operation and Development (OECD)** is an intergovernmental organization founded in 1961 to promote policies that improve the economic and social well-being of people around the world. It consists of 38 member countries, mostly from developed economies, including the United States, most EU countries, Japan, Canada, and Australia. The OECD provides a platform for governments to collaborate on economic, social, and environmental policies that address common challenges such as economic growth, trade, sustainable development, and inequality.
