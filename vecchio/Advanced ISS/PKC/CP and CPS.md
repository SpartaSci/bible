### CP and CPS

The certification policy is outlined in two key documents: the **Certificate Policy (CP)** and the **Certification Practices Statement (CPS)**.

- **Certificate Policy (CP)**: A named set of rules that defines the applicability of a Public Key Certificate ([[Advanced ISS/PKC/Public-key certificate (PKC)|PKC]]) to a particular community (e.g., a Windows Domain) or class of applications (e.g., certificates for Internet electronic mail). The CP specifies common security requirements.

- **Certification Practices Statement (CPS)**: A document that details the practices (procedures) employed by a **Certification Authority (CA)** when issuing a PKC.

A **CP** sets minimum requirements that can be followed by many CAs. For example, a government might have a single CP that must be implemented by all certification providers, mandating a minimum level of identity verification (such as verifying an identity card or password).

A **CPS** contains the specific implementation details used by a single CA. While the CA follows the CP, the CPS allows flexibility in how the requirements are met.
