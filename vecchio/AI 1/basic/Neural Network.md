- **nodes**: process and transform input data, producing an output;
- **layers**: are composed of nodes and are of 3 different types:
	- **input**: layer getting the input data;
	- **hidden**: layers in the middle of the network;
	- **output**: layer providing the final output.
- **synapses**: pass the info between neurons in different layers.
