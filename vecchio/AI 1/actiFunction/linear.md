
The **linear activation function** outputs the input directly without any modification.

Its formula is:

$$
f(x) = x
$$

#### 🔑 Key Points:
- No non-linearity is introduced.
- Often used in the **output layer** for regression tasks.
- Simple and easy to compute.
- Cannot model complex patterns alone, so it's typically combined with hidden layers using non-linear activations.
