**Underfitting** is a symptom of a model being too simple for the problem it is trying to solve. 

It can be noticed due to the high loss on the training set. 

It can be improved by paying more attention to the *pre-processing phase* (e.g., remove noise, evaluate outliers, consider other encoding techniques for categorical features, etc) and to the model engineering phase (e.g., increase the model’s complexity, increase the number of epochs, try with different batch sizes, etc).
