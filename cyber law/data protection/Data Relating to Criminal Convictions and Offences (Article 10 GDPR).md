
### Data Relating to Criminal Convictions and Offences (Article 10 GDPR)

**Article 10 of the GDPR** deals with the processing of **personal data related to criminal convictions and offences**. This category of data requires **specific safeguards** because of its sensitive nature.
- **Specific safeguards** must be in place to protect this type of data, including strong access controls, encryption, and limited retention periods.
- **Limitations**: Processing this data may be restricted based on specific national or EU law provisions, creating a highly regulated environment for such data.
