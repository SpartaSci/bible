
### Sensitive Data / Special Categories of Personal Data (Article 9 GDPR)

**Article 9 of the GDPR** specifies certain categories of personal data as **sensitive** or **special categories of personal data**, which are subject to stricter protection due to the potential for harm or discrimination if misused. These include:

- **Racial or ethnic origin**
- **Political opinions**, **religious** or **philosophical beliefs**
- **Trade union membership**
- **Data concerning health** or data related to a natural person's **sex life** or **sexual orientation**
- **Genetic data** and **biometric data** for the purpose of uniquely identifying a natural person

Processing of these categories is **prohibited** unless specific exceptions apply, such as **explicit consent**, necessary for **employment law**, or for reasons of **substantial public interest**.
