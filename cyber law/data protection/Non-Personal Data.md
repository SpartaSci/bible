### Non-Personal Data

**Regulation (EU) 2018/1807** establishes a framework for the free flow of non-personal data within the EU. It defines **non-personal data** as:

- **“Data other than personal data** as defined in point (1) of Article 4 of Regulation (EU) 2016/679.”

This regulation aims to facilitate the sharing and processing of non-personal data, promoting innovation and economic growth without the constraints of personal data protection laws.
