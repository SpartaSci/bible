

Presenting evidence is crucial for convincing judges and lawyers.

### Guidelines for an Effective Report:
- **Index**: To facilitate navigation.
- **Glossary and Notes**: To explain technical terms.
- **Timelines and Flowcharts**: To clearly illustrate operations.
- **Slides and Photos**: To visually represent data.
- **Video Recordings**: To document the process.

### Key Consideration:
Even with strong evidence, a poorly comprehensible report can jeopardize the case.
