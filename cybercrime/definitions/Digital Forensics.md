
>Digital forensics is a process that begins with **copying data** from an IT system without modifying the original system. 

## Key Steps in Digital Forensics

1. **Data Copying**: 
   - Data is copied from the original IT system to ensure the original remains untouched.
   
2. **Hash Function Verification**:
   - A hash function is used to confirm that the copied data is identical to the original.
   - This step ensures no errors or changes occurred during the copying process.

3. **Data Analysis**:
   - The copied data is analyzed while ensuring that both:
     - The original data.
     - The copied data.  
   Remain unmodified throughout the verification and analysis stages.

## Principles of Digital Forensics: The Big Five

The Council of Europe has defined a set of principles, known as the **Big Five**, which must be followed during digital forensics processes. 

1. **Data Integrity**  
   No action taken should change electronic devices or media, which may subsequently be relied upon in court.

2. **Chain of Custody**  
   An audit trail of all actions taken when handling electronic evidence should be created and preserved.

3. **Specialist Support**  
   If investigations involving search and seizure of electronic evidence, it may be necessary to consult external specialists.

4. **Appropriate Training**  
   First responders must be appropriately trained to be able to search for and seize electronic evidence if no experts are available at the scene.

5. **Legality**  
   The person and agency in charge of the case are responsible for ensuring that the law and the above-listed principles are adhered to.
