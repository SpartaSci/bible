# (Security) Certification

[[SVT/roba inutile/Certification Standards/security assurance and trust|security assurance and trust]]


## Types of Certification

1. **Process Certification**: 
   - This type of certification focuses on the properties of the development process rather than the specific properties of the system itself.

2. **Product Certification**: 
   - This involves formal attestation, which is a document certifying specific security properties of a system.
   
   
The formal attestation must be produced by an **accredited third-party independent party** recognized as a valid authority.
This third-party must not be involved in the development of the product.

### Requirements for Product Certification

- **Evidence**: The certification process includes some form of evidence supporting the claims made about the product's security properties.
- **Recognized Criteria**: The certification is performed according to recognized criteria, known as **certification standards**.

### Examples of Certification Standards

- **Common Criteria**: A widely recognized standard for product certification.
- **System Security Engineering Capability Maturity Model**: A standard for process certification.
