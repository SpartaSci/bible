
Relying solely on software to protect other software is generally not a good idea, as software can fail or be compromised. To ensure robust protection, **hardware support** is necessary.

- **Root-of-Trust (RoT)** should be part of the **Trusted Computing Base (TCB)** because hardware, in the end, is always operated by software or firmware.
  
- The **TCB** must be as minimal as possible to reduce the attack surface and enhance security.
