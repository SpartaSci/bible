

Public extensions are those defined in the standard and that everyone should understand. They are divided into four classes:

- Additional information about the [[Advanced ISS/PKC/Key and Policy Information|Key and Policy Information]]
- Additional attributes with respect to the [[Advanced ISS/PKC/Certificate Subject and Certificate Issuer Attributes|Certificate Subject and Certificate Issuer Attributes]]
- [[Advanced ISS/PKC/Certificate Path Constraints|Constraints about the certificate path]]: the sequence of certificates from the root to this place
- [[Advanced ISS/PKC/CRL Distribution Point|CRL Distribution Point]]: extensions to identify the location where the CRL can be obtained

We often refer to **EE = End Entity**, which means the one at the end of the certification hierarchy that possesses the public and private key.
